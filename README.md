# Forensic Case Reconstruction: The "Detective's Mindset"
### 🕵️ Focus: Logic, Pattern Recognition & Investigative Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
While not a traditional coding project, this case reconstruction represents the foundational mindset of a Data Scientist: the ability to aggregate disparate, messy "data points" (forensic evidence) to reconstruct a sequence of events and identify a definitive conclusion.

## 🚀 The Challenge
Analyzed a complex crime scene involving multiple types of forensic evidence to determine the culpability of suspects and the timeline of events.

## 📊 Evidence "Data Points" Analyzed
* **Biological Evidence:** Analyzed DNA and SAK (Sexual Assault Kit) results to establish physical links between individuals.
* **Trace Evidence:** Evaluated glass transfer patterns on clothing and tools to determine points of entry and exit.
* **Pattern Analysis:** Interpreted blood spatter distribution on walls and footwear to reconstruct the dynamics of a violent altercation.
* **Toxicology:** Factored in substance presence (Alcohol/Cocaine) to establish potential behavioral motives and state of mind.

## 💡 Why This Matters in Data Science
Data Science is essentially "digital forensics." This project demonstrates my ability to:
1. **Connect the Dots:** Link independent variables to a single outcome.
2. **Eliminate Noise:** Distinguish between coincidental evidence and causal evidence.
3. **Structured Reporting:** Present a definitive narrative backed by objective data rather than intuition.
