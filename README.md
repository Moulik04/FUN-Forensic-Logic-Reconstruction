# Forensic Data Reconstruction: Case Board

### 1. Project Overview
Performed a systematic reconstruction of a complex crime scene by synthesizing physical evidence to determine the sequence of events and identify the primary aggressor.

### 2. Dataset
Heterogeneous forensic evidence including:
* Glass fragment analysis (interior vs. exterior).
* Blood spatter patterns on walls and clothing.
* Toxicology reports (Alcohol/Cocaine levels) and Sexual Assault Kit (SAK) data.

### 3. Approach
* **Techniques:** Deductive reasoning and physical evidence correlation. I used glass distribution to prove forced entry from within and blood spatter to confirm the suspect’s presence during the struggle.
* **Tools:** Case Board visualization and narrative reconstruction.

### 4. Results
* **Insights:** Physical evidence (glass and blood) directly contradicted the suspect's statement, proving he acted alone.
* **Outcome:** Created a definitive timeline that linked the forensic data to the criminal acts, excluding the possibility of collusion.

### 5. Key Learnings
This project sharpened my **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)** skills. In technical terms, this is exactly like debugging a system failure, you have a "crashed" state (the crime scene) and must look at the "logs" (forensic evidence) to reconstruct exactly what happened. It taught me how to build a narrative backed 100% by data, not assumptions.
